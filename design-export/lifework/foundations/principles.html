Principles & Spacing

Premium, minimal, clinical — but warm and human. Seed is the typographic reference, ARMRA the benefit-breadth reference, Timeline the clinical-data reference.

  • One primary message per viewport. Generous whitespace (80–112px) between major sections.
  • Hairline 1px borders instead of drop shadows for separation and elevation.
  • Red is earned, not decorative: CTAs, active states, proof numbers, eyebrows only.
  • Clinical charts look like real study data — dotted gridlines, error bars, one delta annotation. Never exaggerated marketing graphics.
  • Photography carries the editorial weight; avoid bordered-card overload.
  • Comparison cells are nuanced (check / “Varies” / “Partial” / —), never giant green checks vs red X marks.
  • Compliance: every structure/function claim carries a trailing *; review bodies stay as [verified customer review] until real ones exist.
